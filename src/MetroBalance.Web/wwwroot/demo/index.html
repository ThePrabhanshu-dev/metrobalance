Where do I find my Smart Card number?
  1. Look at the front of your card — the number is printed on it.
  2. Use the 12-digit number printed on the card (it may be shown in groups like 8177 7696 0017).
  3. Type it below. Spaces are fine, we ignore them.

    What this means for you

    A short, plain-English summary of the screen above.

    • Money left on your card — the rupees you can still travel with.
    • Every time you added money — your top-ups, with date and amount.
    • Every trip and what it cost — station to station, with the fare deducted.
    • Last updated — when this information was last refreshed, so you always know how fresh it is.

    Plain words: if the balance looks lower than you expect, the difference is normally a journey fare that was already deducted. The journey list shows exactly which one.

    Questions people ask

    Short answers, no jargon.

    Do I need to create an account or log in?
    No. Type the card number and press one button. There is no sign-up, no password and no login of any kind. That is also why nothing about you is stored.
    Where is the number printed on my card?
    On the front of the card you will find a 12-digit Smart Card number. On the back you will find the 10 or 11 digit Engraved ID, which is the number you use for online top-ups.
    Is the balance shown here my real balance?
    In this public demo, no — every figure is clearly marked DEMO DATA and is a simulated sample. DMRC does not currently publish an online card balance service, so no website can show a live balance. With DMRC authorisation, the same screen would read the real balance from the official source.
    How much does it cost me?
    Nothing. The application uses only free, open-source technology and has no paid service behind it. Running it for DMRC riders would also be free of charge.
    Does it work on my phone?
    Yes. The screen adapts to any phone, tablet or laptop, and loads without an app download.
    My card is blocked or expired — what happens?
    The screen tells you the card status straight away, together with the official steps to get it reactivated at a Customer Care counter.
