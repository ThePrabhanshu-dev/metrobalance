## 4. Published facts that shape the proposal

### 4.1 DMRC's own position on online balances (the key quote)

Source: <https://www.dmrcsmartcard.com/FAQ.aspx>, section *"Balance Related Queries"* — verbatim:

> **Can I check my card balance online?**
> **No, you cannot check your card balance online.**
> Visit any station and show your card on the TR CUM AVM (Installed Near Customer Care/EFO at all DMRC Metro stations
> including Airport Express Line) by touching Inquiry Button on AVM Screen.

**Implication:** there is no public card-balance API or feed. Any live feature needs a **new, authorised** data
interface from DMRC — it cannot be built on top of anything DMRC already publishes. Say this explicitly in the proposal;
it turns an apparent weakness into a credible, well-researched ask.

### 4.2 Other verbatim FAQ rules (source: <https://www.dmrcsmartcard.com/FAQ.aspx>)

* *"DMRC will not be liable if you enter incorrect Engraved ID… this may lead to recharge of another card."*
* *"…once the online payment become successful and a top-up has been created in DMRC system for the smartcard, it will be
  considered a 'service rendered'… No chargeback or refund request will be applicable."*
* A passenger must present the card at an AVM to avail an online top-up **within 180 days**; after that the top-up is
  non-refundable (w.e.f. 01-03-2024).
* Maximum value maintained on a card: **₹3,000.00** or as per DMRC business rule.
* *"No, there is no discount available for online recharge of any value."*
* Online recharge is not possible between **11:45 PM and 12:15 AM** (daily maintenance window).
* *"all the smart cards issued by DMRC are anonymous"* — DMRC does not know who owns a card. *(A direct support for a
  card-number-only, no-login enquiry model.)*

### 4.3 DMRC already permits named third-party channels

Source: <https://delhimetrorail.com/faq-tickets-fare>

> *"For DMRC Lines, Mobile QR Ticket can be purchased online through Delhi Metro Sarthi App, PayTM, One Delhi, PhonePe,
> Amazon apps and WhatsApp chatbot… Virtual Smart Card can be purchased online through Delhi Metro Sarthi App."*

DMRC's official app is the **Delhi Metro Sarthi App** (`com.sraoss.dmrc`). Third-party distribution is therefore not
unprecedented — the ask is a **formal, authorised arrangement**, not a favour.

### 4.4 Hyperlinking & copyright clauses (source: <https://delhimetrorail.com/privacy-policy>)

> *"DMRC without any prior permission do not permit anyone to load/hyperlink any page of the website to any other
> website. DMRC also do not permit any app/blog or any social media pages to be hyperlinked to DMRC's website…"*
> *"DMRC will not sell, rent, publish or trade any user's personal information to any individual or agency/organisation
> under any circumstances."*

**Implication:** do not link to DMRC from the app, and do not use the DMRC logo/name without written permission. The
demonstration build already avoids both and carries an explicit "not affiliated" notice.

### 4.5 The most relevant published hook: DMRC Innovation Policy (20 Nov 2025)

<https://backend.delhimetrorail.com/documents/9663/DMRC-INNOVATION_POLICY-20.11.2025.pdf> (linked from
<https://delhimetrorail.com/pages/en/dmrc-policies>)

This is the single most likely published document under which a technology/startup proposal is considered. **Mention it
by name in the e-mail** — it shows the proposal was routed to the right desk. *(The PDF text could not be extracted in
this environment; read it yourself before quoting it.)*
